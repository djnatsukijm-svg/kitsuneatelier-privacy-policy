Privacy Policy

Shop: KitsuneAtelierShop (Etsy) · Last updated: 2026-08-14

KitsuneAtelierShop ("we", "us", "our") operates a shop on Etsy.com selling Japanese culture and seasonal-themed keychains and related products. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and which third-party services we share it with in connection with fulfilling and marketing orders.

1. Information We Collect

When you place an order through our Etsy shop, Etsy provides us with information necessary to fulfill that order, which may include:

  • Name and shipping address
  • Email address (for order-related communication)
  • Order details (items purchased, quantities, personalization notes)

We do not collect payment card information directly — all payments are processed by Etsy, Inc.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To process, produce, and ship your order
  • To communicate with you about your order (confirmations, shipping updates, support)
  • To comply with legal and tax obligations related to selling on Etsy

3. Third-Party Services We Use

To operate this shop, we work with the following third-party services, each of which has its own privacy policy governing how they handle data:

  • Etsy, Inc. — the marketplace platform through which orders are placed and payments are processed. (Etsy Privacy Policy)
  • Printify, Inc. — our print-on-demand fulfillment partner. Order and shipping information necessary to produce and ship your item is shared with Printify. (Printify Privacy Policy)
  • Buffer (via Pinterest) — used for scheduling promotional content about our products on Pinterest. No customer order data is shared with Buffer or Pinterest; this use is limited to marketing our public product listings. (Buffer Privacy Policy)

4. Data Retention

We retain order information only as long as necessary to fulfill orders, provide customer support, and comply with applicable tax and legal recordkeeping requirements.

5. Your Rights

You may contact us at any time to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, subject to any legal obligations that require us to retain certain records (e.g., order/tax history).

6. Cookies

This shop operates on Etsy's platform. Cookie usage on Etsy.com is governed by Etsy's own Privacy Policy. We do not independently set tracking cookies outside of Etsy's platform.
